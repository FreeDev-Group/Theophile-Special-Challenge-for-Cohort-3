Use Case: Review Past Feedback
=================================
## All previously submitted surveys

As expected, all completed surveys are visible on the site in accordance with the use case. I am able to see all of them; nothing is lost.
![](./images/All%20previously%20submitted%20surveys.png)

## About date on surveys

It is indicated that students can see the list of questionnaires they have completed along with the date, but this is not the case for me. I can see the completed surveys, but I cannot find the completion date.
But I also cannot find anywhere to filter my data.
![](./images/About%20date%20on%20surveys.png)

## The information displayed

In line with the requirement that 'The information displayed (answers, timestamps) must match exactly what was submitted,' I noticed that some of the answers I submitted last time are missing from the site. Based on the user case, this appears to be a bug and needs to be fixed.
![](./images/No%20questions%20found.png)

## Navigation back to the main menu

After finishing checking my completed surveys, the home button was very visible and easy to access to get back to the homepage.
![](./images/Home%20page.png)

## Additional observations

- It would be helpful to have a filter to sort the surveys by date.
